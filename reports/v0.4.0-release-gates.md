1. **Live Container Test**: Start Docker Desktop and execute `.\tests\run_integration.ps1`.
2. **Multi-Peer Mesh Test**: Boot 2 or 3 `libp2p-node` daemons and trigger cross-peer sync.
3. **Chaos Recovery Validation**: Terminate `api-server` or `git-server` during active push/pull and verify crash recovery.
